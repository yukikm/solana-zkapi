WHAT HAPPENS UNDERNEATH

A simple flow. Three key ideas.

01 / FUND

Fund your AI usage with USDC

Pay for AI from funds deposited in a Vault on Solana.

02 / PROVE

Prove access with ZK

Prove you have enough balance and meet the access conditions, then call the API.

03 / SETTLE

Check the receipt. Withdraw the rest.

The live app verifies signed receipts and lets you withdraw your balance after settlement.
